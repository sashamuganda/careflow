# careflow
Hospital side and patient side platform available on mobile through app and even using SMS and online through a web platform that helps guide an unwell patient through the healthcare experience. A patient opens the app, describes their symptoms and a triage system powered by AI processes this taking into consideration patient records and history to assess the urgency and suggests the right department whereby the hospital side/the staff side has a live dashboard that shows how patients are moving and let's doctors update their availability in real time to avoid and track bottlenecks. It is a single, simple, smart platform that connects smart queueing, AI, triage and hospital management in one smooth flow.
